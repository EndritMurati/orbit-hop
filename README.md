# Orbit Hop

A one-tap arcade game. You're a tiny moon circling a planet: hop between the inner and outer orbit to dodge debris and grab stars while the spin speeds up.

## Play

Open `index.html` in any browser. No build step or dependencies.

## Controls

- **Tap / click / Space**: hop orbits
- **P** or **Esc**: pause
- **M**: toggle sound

Your best score is saved in your browser.
