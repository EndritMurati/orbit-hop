# Bored Arcade

Two small browser games, no build step or dependencies. Open `index.html` to pick one.

## Hornet Storm (`hornet-storm/`)

A retro pixel-art space shooter in the style of 80s arcade games. Enemy formations swoop in and dive at you, a giant boss shows up every third stage, and there are power-ups, chiptune music, a CRT screen filter and an arcade-style high score table.

- **Arrows / WASD**: move
- **Space / Z**: fire
- **P / Esc**: pause, **M**: sound, **C**: CRT filter
- On a phone: drag to fly, firing is automatic

## Orbit Hop (`orbit-hop/`)

A one-tap arcade game. You're a tiny moon circling a planet: hop between the inner and outer orbit to dodge debris and grab stars while the spin speeds up.

- **Tap / click / Space**: hop orbits
- **P / Esc**: pause, **M**: sound

High scores are saved in your browser.
